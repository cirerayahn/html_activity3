  <h1>Angela's Cinnamon Roll Recipe</h1>
  <hr>

  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>
      For the dough:
      <ul>
        <li>¾ cup warm milk</li>
        <li>2 ¼ teaspoons yeast</li>
        <li>¼ cup granulated sugar</li>
        <li>1 egg plus 1 egg yolk</li>
        <li>¼ cup butter</li>
        <li>3 cups bread flour</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>
      For the filling:
      <ul>
        <li>⅔ cup dark brown sugar</li>
        <li>1 ½ tablespoons ground cinnamon</li>
        <li>¼ cup butter</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>

  <hr>

  <h2>Instructions</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>Mix the milk with the yeast, sugar, and eggs.</li>
    <li>Melt the butter and add it to the mixture.</li>
    <li>Add in the flour and mix until combined into a dough.</li>
    <li>Knead the dough for 10 minutes.</li>
    <li>
      Transfer the dough into a large bowl and cover with plastic wrap.
      <br>
      Leave it somewhere warm to rise for 2 hours, or until doubled in size.
    </li>
    <li>After the dough has doubled in size, roll it out into a large rectangle.</li>
    <li>
      Prepare the filling:
      <ul>
        <li>Melt the butter for the filling.</li>
        <li>Mix in the dark brown sugar and cinnamon.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Spread the filling evenly onto the dough.</li>
    <li>Roll the dough into a swiss roll (tight log).</li>
    <li>Cut the roll into 3 cm sections and place them flat into a baking tray.</li>
    <li>
      Bake the rolls:
      <br>
      Pre-heat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
      <br>
      Bake for 20–25 minutes until lightly brown.
    </li>
  </ol>
